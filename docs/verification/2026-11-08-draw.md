# Draw presentation checkpoint — 2026-11-08

This supersedes the placeholder Secret-presentation limitation in the earlier recovery checkpoint. It does not declare the full recovery specification complete.

## Implemented

- A dedicated full-screen draw sequence, rendered by `DrawSequence`, shows every drawn oven in order. Each oven enters from beyond the actual viewport, rotates through the center with a rarity-colored star/trail, and exits before the next step begins.
- Every Secret draw adds its own 6.4-second cinematic, including duplicates in the same batch. Results appear only after the final flight and cinematic finish.
- The original cookie bitmap is partitioned into 57 source-image tiles. They separate, rotate/mix and reassemble. The complete bitmap replaces the assembled tiles to avoid fractional-pixel seam artifacts.
- A rotating, continuous rainbow frame and nine staggered slash lines accompany the cookie. The final reveal retains the large cookie, localized oven name and ×95 multiplier.
- Reduced motion uses a stationary cookie and a short opacity reveal; no flying pieces or slash field.
- Reanimated 4.5.1 and Worklets 0.10.1 match Expo SDK 57. Native motion uses shared-value transforms/opacity. Timeline completion crosses to React only at step boundaries; phase changes cross only four times per cinematic.
- Development-only `?preview=draw` mounts the production presentation with known fixtures. It imports no game store and does not modify saved progress. Its entry is gated by both `__DEV__` and the web platform.
